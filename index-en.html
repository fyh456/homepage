About Me

I am Yuhang Fan, a graduate student in Physics at the School of Physical Science and Engineering, Tongji University, specializing in optics. I am fortunate to conduct my research under the supervision of Prof. Xiao Deng.

My group is affiliated with the National Center for Industrial Metrology and Testing of IC Micro/Nano Inspection Equipment (Shanghai). We combine metrology, precision instrumentation, and data analysis to build reliable “nanoscale rulers” for advanced semiconductor manufacturing. Related work undertaken by my supervisor and team received the First Prize of the 2025 State Scientific and Technological Progress Award.

Research Interests

Nanoscale Film-Thickness Measurement and Characterization

To determine whether different methods produce consistent and defensible film-thickness results, I measured an approximately 11 nm single-layer SiO₂ film through three independently traceable routes: standard-calibrated ellipsometry, Si(111)-referenced TEM, and self-traceable-grating-referenced TEM. Metrological compatibility assessment and uncertainty-weighted combination yielded a reference thickness of 11.45 ± 0.10 nm (k=2).

KEYWORDSMultiple Traceability RoutesMetrological CompatibilityReference Value

Image and Signal Processing for Optical Metrology

Using thin-film interfaces and grating structures as experimental objects, I combine half-intensity analysis, Canny edge detection, gradient extrema, FFT, and range-based inversion into an automated workflow for preprocessing, feature extraction, thickness and pitch calculation, and batch export, while exploring transfer to other microscopy-image tasks.

KEYWORDSInterface ExtractionPitch CalculationBatch Processing

Ellipsometric Modeling and Uncertainty Propagation

To understand how nanoscale film thickness is recovered from changes in light polarization, I model the relationships between the ellipsometric parameters ψ and Δ, optical constants, film thickness, wavelength, and incidence angle. I then track how input errors propagate and amplify during inversion, providing a basis for measurement optimization, instrument design, and uncertainty evaluation.

KEYWORDSψ/Δ ModelingSensitivity AnalysisError Propagation
Current Focus

I aim to address nanoscale measurement challenges in advanced integrated-circuit manufacturing by using metrological methodology and error theory to improve the reliability of instruments and results, while developing systematic and automated tools that reduce human bias and uncertainty in defect and dimensional analysis.
